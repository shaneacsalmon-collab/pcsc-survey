Strategic Plan 2026–2031

Member & Stakeholder Survey
📅  Responses due Thursday 25 June 2026

Your elected PCSC Executive Committee needs your assistance. As a requirement of the Fee for Service Agreement between the PCSC and the Shire of Manjimup, the Executive Committee is developing the 2026–2031 PCSC Strategic Plan.

The Strategic Plan will provide clear direction, assist in setting priorities and allocating resources effectively, and ensure the Executive Committee works towards common goals for all PCSC Clubs, Community Groups and Club members.

Please complete the short questionnaire below. Your input is genuinely valued — every response helps shape the Club's direction for the next five years. Thank you in anticipation.

— John Bendotti (President), Jodie Omodei (Vice-President), Karen Fisher (Treasurer), Mari Sebire, Shane Salmon & Hazel Cole (Secretary)
